2. Core App Workflow & Local Processing

Our core functionality is designed with a Privacy-First Approach:

  • 100% On-Device Local Processing: All image conversions, format modifications (PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, BMP), PDF rendering, custom dimension adjustments, and quality controls occur entirely locally on your device.
  • No File Uploads: Your photos, files, and generated documents are NEVER uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any external servers or cloud services.
  • Local Output: Converted files are processed locally and saved directly to your device's storage (e.g., Pictures/ConvertedImage or Download/ConvertedImages/) only upon your request.

3. Permissions Used

We adhere strictly to the principle of Data Minimization. Permissions used include:

  • Storage Access (Android 9 and below): Required to write/read converted images and PDFs.
  • Photo Picker / Scoped Media Access (Android 10+): Utilizes standard system MediaStore APIs to let you select images for conversion and export converted files. No broad file system access is requested.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App integrates Software Development Kits (SDKs) provided by Huawei Mobile Services (HMS):

  • Huawei Ads SDK (HwAds): To serve contextual advertisements. HMS Ads may process non-personally identifiable device identifiers (OAID) and network information.
  • Huawei In-App Purchases (HMS IAP): To process digital purchases (such as "Remove Ads"). All billing is handled directly by Huawei's secure payment system. We do not collect or store financial credentials.
