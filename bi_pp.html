2. Advertisements & In-App Purchases (Huawei SDKs)

  • Huawei Petal Ads SDK: The free version of this App displays advertisements provided by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK. To serve relevant ad content and prevent fraud, Huawei Petal Ads may collect anonymous device identifiers (such as OAID), network connection indicators, and basic hardware parameters.
  • Huawei In-App Purchases (IAP) SDK: We offer an optional "Remove Ads" digital item using the Huawei In-App Purchases SDK. All financial transactions and order validations are processed securely through the official Huawei Mobile Services (HMS) infrastructure. We do not store or access your payment credentials.

3. Background Service & Local Data Storage

  • Real-Time Monitoring Service: The App uses an active background service to continuously measure battery performance indicators, including current (mA), voltage, wattage, and charging stability.
  • Local Analytics Storage: All battery health logs, daily usage trends, and diagnostic statistics are saved strictly on your local device storage. We do not transfer or sync your battery analytics to external servers.
  • Ongoing Notification Control: A persistent status notification is required for reliable background monitoring. You can enable or disable this background tracking service at any time directly from the App Settings screen.

4. Optional System Permissions

To deliver accurate battery monitoring during long standby sessions or after rebooting, the App may request the following optional system permissions:

  • Ignore Battery Optimization: Recommended to prevent the Android operating system from closing the background monitoring process.
  • Auto-Start: Recommended to allow the background service to automatically resume collecting battery statistics after your device restarts.

Note: These permissions are entirely optional. Denying them will not limit your access to the main foreground screens of the App.
