We do not share any data with third-party analytical or advertising networks. The application operates without requiring sensitive device permissions, such as access to your location, camera, contacts, storage, or microphone.

Because no personal data or usage metrics are gathered or transmitted to remote databases, your data security risk is non-existent. Your content remains completely contained within your local hardware environment.
